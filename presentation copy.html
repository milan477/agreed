The problem
Both sides hide more than they share

The insight
When preferences are fully mapped, the trade space expands
Agents surface what each side actually values — price, timing, terms, relationships — making every tradeoff visible.
The outcome
The best deal is the one neither side can improve on
Agreed finds the agreement where both parties gain as much as possible — simultaneously.
